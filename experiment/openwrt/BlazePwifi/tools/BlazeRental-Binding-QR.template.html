BlazeRental Standard Binding QR

Already-installed phone binding only. BlazeRental must already be installed and the operator must open its QR binding scanner.

Lower-security mode: this QR does not provision Device Owner, does not download an APK, and contains no Android enterprise provisioning fields.

Ready.


Already-installed phone flow
  1. Install/open BlazeRental normally.
  2. Open the on-device BlazePwifi binding scanner.
  3. Generate a fresh one-time enrollment token in BlazePwifi Admin.
  4. Scan this binding QR.
For factory-reset Device Owner setup, use the separate BlazeRental Device Owner Provisioning QR tool instead.
