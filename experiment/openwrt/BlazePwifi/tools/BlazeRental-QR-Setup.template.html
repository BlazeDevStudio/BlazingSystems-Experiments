BlazeRental Device Owner Provisioning QR

Factory-reset Device Owner provisioning only. This is separate from the standard already-installed binding QR. Generate the enrollment token first in BlazePwifi Admin → Rental phones.

Ready.


Fresh-phone flow
  1. Factory reset or use a new/unprovisioned Android phone.
  2. At the Android welcome screen, trigger enterprise QR provisioning (commonly tap the welcome area six times; OEM behavior varies).
  3. Connect to internet if the QR does not include Wi-Fi.
  4. Scan this QR. Android downloads BlazeRental.apk, verifies its checksum, and provisions it as Device Owner.


For Full BlazePwifi's default self-signed HTTPS certificate, copy the certificate SHA-256 shown/generated by the server. A CA-trusted HTTPS server may leave the pin blank. Prefer BlazePwifi Admin's server-generated Device Owner QR because it fills the local certificate pin automatically.
A hardware/recovery factory wipe can remove any third-party DPC. Re-scan a new enrollment QR after such a wipe.
